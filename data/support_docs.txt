Product Overview:
TaskFlow is a cloud-based project management platform that helps teams manage tasks, deadlines, and collaboration.

Account Creation:
Users can create an account using an email and password or by signing in with Google Single Sign-On.

Login Issues:
If a user cannot log in, they should click on the "Forgot Password" link to reset their password.

Subscription Plans:
TaskFlow offers three plans: Free, Pro, and Enterprise. Subscriptions are billed monthly.

Refund Policy:
Users are eligible for a refund within 14 days of purchase for monthly subscriptions.
Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after approval.

Payment Failures:
If a payment fails, users are notified by email and have 7 days to update their payment method before the account is downgraded.

Account Cancellation:
Users can cancel their subscription at any time from the account settings page.

Account Deletion:
Users can request permanent account deletion from the account settings. All data is deleted within 30 days.

Team Management:
Pro and Enterprise users can invite team members and assign roles such as Admin or Member.

Security:
TaskFlow encrypts data in transit and at rest to protect user information.

Support Contact:
Users can contact support via email or in-app chat for additional assistance.
